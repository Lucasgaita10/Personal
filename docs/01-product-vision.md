# 01 — Product Vision

Stone Gate is the **investment committee intelligence system** for institutional and private real estate advisory firms.

## North star

> Compress weeks of diligence into days, with every claim sourced and every assumption challenged.

## Three product principles

1. **Source-traceable by default.** Every quantitative or qualitative claim is anchored to a document chunk, page number, and chunk id. The audit trail is the product.
2. **Adversarial intelligence.** The system always argues both sides — the IC Writer drafts the case for, the IC Challenger drafts the case against, and both are surfaced.
3. **Local-first, cloud-ready.** Confidential data rooms never leave the firm's perimeter unless explicitly authorized. The architecture supports a single-tenant cloud deployment when needed.

## Personas

- **Principal** — wants the IC memo + a 90-second view of every active deal.
- **Investment Analyst** — wants chat copilot, gap surfacing, and one-click memo drafts.
- **Acquisitions Director** — wants the pipeline kanban and stage-gate scores.
- **Compliance** — wants the audit log and source traceability.

## The core moments

1. Drag-and-drop the data room → opportunity is classified, indexed, and a first-pass thesis is drafted in minutes.
2. Open Insights → ask "what kills this deal?" → get a sourced devil's-advocate answer.
3. Run a recession scenario → IRR, MOIC, DSCR refresh in real time with a waterfall view.
4. Click "Generate IC memo" → produce a Stone Gate-branded PDF in 60 seconds.
